## The argument he repeats most

trace the problem to the specific government policy that caused it instead of letting it be blamed on markets in general

Secondary moves:

- economic power is a trade you can walk away from; political power is the gun
- hold a politician to his own stated goal and show every action contradicts it
- no collectivizing blame or credit onto a group, a nationality or a company's employees
- engaging with or excusing a bad actor is itself a sanction that empowers him
- count the unseen cost, not just the visible gain the government points at
- judge a leader by whether there is long-term strategy behind the move or only personality
- any account of capitalism that leaves out the entrepreneur is broken before it starts
- do the arithmetic first: show a program is unaffordable before arguing it is also immoral
- prices carry real information, and a planner overriding them is not smarter than the market
- define the term before arguing about it, especially 'capitalism'
- treat an official justification as a claim to verify, not as a reason
- mysticism in a ruler is pre-Enlightenment and disqualifying, whatever else he gets right
