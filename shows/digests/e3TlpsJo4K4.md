# Hegseth; Iran; Russia; JDVance; Debt; Oil; Golden Shares; Founders; Achievement | Yaron Brook Show

## Topics

- Hegseth and the Pentagon shakeup — largest topic — He credits Hegseth for opposing "just war theory" and purging DEI but calls him unqualified and unstrategic, arguing the military needs leaner, tech/drone/AI-focused leadership rather than Hegseth's "warrior" physical-fitness fixation, and blames him for the drawn-out, poorly planned Iran war and munitions shortages; he also flags Army Secretary Dan Driscoll's resignation as evidence of internal dysfunction.
- Iran war status — large — He argues the war is winnable easily with real commitment, notes the Navy has only recently (after 6 months) restored partial oil flow through the Strait of Hormuz, sees Iran's missile capability degrading (high failure rates) but still dangerous via new Russian-aided cruise missile tech, and says Trump has no coherent exit strategy, wavering between hoping sanctions topple the regime and wanting to declare victory and leave.
- Russia/Ukraine — medium — He says Russia is pounding Ukraine with new fast jet-powered drones (derived from Iranian Shahed tech) while Ukraine lacks Patriot missiles and comparable air defense; he condemns Trump for calling Russia an ally and Canada "nasty," and for Bessent blaming Ukraine (not Russia) for oil-price pressure from energy-infrastructure strikes.
- JD Vance's religiosity — medium — He plays clips of Vance framing world affairs through spiritual/mystical feelings (sensing "dark energy," seeing providence in the Trump assassination attempt, entertaining the Antichrist and "end times") and argues this pre-Enlightenment mysticism is disqualifying and dangerous in a potential president.
- Venezuela oil deal — medium — He blasts the Trump administration's deal granting NABP (led by a businessman he says is documented as corrupt) development rights to Venezuelan oil fields with the US government taking a 35% equity stake, below-market purchase rights, and board control, calling it socialism/industrial policy dressed as privatization, though he grants it geopolitically pulls Venezuela from Russia/China's orbit and away from reliance on Canadian oil.
- Global bond markets / debt — small — He tracks rising long-term yields worldwide (Japan's 10-year at a 30-year high, weak yen, high US 10-year) as the likely source of the next financial crisis.
- Immigrant founders of US unicorns — small — He cites data that immigrants (especially Indians and, per capita, Israelis by far the highest rate) founded a huge share of billion-dollar US companies, arguing this makes the case against restricting legal immigration.
- Ordinary Abundance website / progress — small — He praises a website contrasting historical wonder (1888 utopian predictions, first electric-lit towns) with today's mundane abundance, as a reminder to feel awe at material progress.

## Running threads

- Iran war: continues tracking Hormuz shipping-volume recovery, missile failure rates, and lack of a US exit strategy, building on prior weeks' claims that the war was winnable if pursued seriously from week one.

- JD Vance disqualification file: he says he is deliberately compiling recurring video evidence of Vance's mysticism to preempt "lesser evil" arguments for voting for him.

## Moves

- Judges leaders by whether they show genuine long-term strategic thinking versus reactive, personality-driven, or ideologically-motivated decisions.
- Treats mysticism/religion in politics as a slippery slope: any belief in divine intervention in daily events is "pre-Enlightenment" and inherently threatens rational governance.
- Uses "gratitude to achievers" as his standard response to envy/resentment arguments, tied to an objectivist framing that genetic gift plus effort equals earned achievement.
- Frames government ownership stakes in private companies as a bipartisan long-term danger, warning that tools built by one side (Trump's equity stakes) will be weaponized by the other (Democrats using them for ESG/DEI).
- Consistently distinguishes "elites" (legitimate specialized knowledge) from "wrong/untrustworthy elites," rejecting anti-elitism as anti-mind while criticizing the current elite class's track record.
