# What Yaron wants from the brief

Your standing instructions. Every brief from now on follows them.

Write them as plain sentences, one per line, **below the line at the bottom of
this note**. They are read by the steps that choose which stories reach the
brief and the steps that write it, for both the articles and the X lists.

Two ways to add one:

- tell Claude: "add to my preferences, I never want celebrity leads"
- or type a line below the rule yourself

Everything above the rule is this note and is ignored. Everything below it is
an instruction. Nothing below it is fine: the brief runs as it always has.

Examples of the kind of thing that works. Copy one below the rule to try it:

- Never lead with a celebrity story.
- I don't use the crime ones.
- More energy policy, especially anything on grid costs.
- Keep the leads shorter.
