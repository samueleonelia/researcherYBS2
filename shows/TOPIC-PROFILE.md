## The argument he repeats most

take the problem people blame on capitalism and trace it to the specific government policy that caused it

Secondary moves:

- economic power is a trade you can walk away from; political power is the gun
- go look at what happened every time it was tried: the USSR, Mao, Venezuela
- ask who bears the risk and who pays the wages before any profit exists
- make the critic define his terms before arguing about them
- any account of the economy that leaves out the entrepreneur is broken
- hold the policy against its own stated goal and watch it contradict itself
- count the unseen cost, not just the visible gain
- apply the standard evenly to left and right, to Israel and to Russia
- let prices carry the information; central planning has no feedback
